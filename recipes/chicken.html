Baked Chicken Legs

Description

Baked Chicken Legs are a delicious and easy-to-make dish featuring tender, juicy chicken with beautifully crispy, golden-brown skin. The chicken is coated in olive oil and a flavorful blend of seasonings, creating a savory taste with aromatic spices that enhance the natural flavor of the meat. Baking the chicken in the oven allows the outside to become crispy while keeping the inside moist and succulent.

Perfect for a simple family dinner or a satisfying weekend meal, these baked chicken legs require minimal preparation and use everyday ingredients. They pair wonderfully with roasted potatoes, steamed vegetables, rice, or a fresh green salad, making them a versatile option for any occasion. Serve them hot straight from the oven for a comforting, flavorful meal that is easy to prepare and enjoyable for everyone.

Ingredients

  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon poultry seasoning
  • 1/4 teaspoon paprika
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 pounds chicken legs

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees. Line baking sheet with foil and lightly spray with nonstick cooking spray and set aside.
  2. In a small bowl, stir together all seasonings and spices.
  3. Open package of chicken legs and pat dry with paper towels. Place onto prepared baking sheet and rub with olive oil. Sprinkle with seasonings on all sides.
  4. Bake, uncovered for 40-45 minutes or until browned and meat thermometer registers 165 degrees or higher. Serve warm.
